Target. The tag effect did not survive replication on hard problems and the fine-tuned generators sit below their base model, so the SFT line cannot carry the paper as it stands. What holds is the verifier itself: refutation as a correctness signal, trajectory selection by real Python verdicts (+.046 on 260 problems), and routed search that raises verified content. Applied to the un-fine-tuned base model's own samples, the verifier beats a random pick but not majority voting, so its niche is where agreement is unavailable: accepting or rejecting a single solution, or free-form answers that do not cluster. That is a workshop paper as it stands.
