Verifier Annotation Desk

Label each reasoning step with the verifier that should check it, and whether the step is right.
Label guide

python: computable. Concrete arithmetic, algebra with specific values, counting, a numeric result a short program can recompute.

lean: formalisable. A general identity, inequality, property or deduction best stated as a theorem and proved.

evidence: a factual claim to be checked against supplied documents or retrieved sources.

judge: needs a model or human judgement that none of the above can give: an interpretation, a modelling choice, a commonsense inference.

none: nothing to verify: a plan, a definition, a restatement, an assumption.

Keys: 1–5 set the verifier of the focused step, c / w / u set correctness, j / k move between steps.
